One time, about 5 minutes

Set up the CMA tool.

You need two things: your own Flexmls username and password, and your own Claude account. Do the steps in order. Each one says exactly what to click, what you should see, and what to do if it looks different.

  1. Open the studio inside Claude
    1. Sign in at claude.ai with your own account.
    2. Open the Marketing Studio link Elliot shared with you. Do not use a downloaded copy.

    You should see: the studio inside Claude, with this guide button on the CMA tab.

    If it says the page is private or you cannot open it: ask Elliot to share the studio with your email address.

  2. Turn on AI access in Flexmls
    1. Sign in to Flexmls as usual.
    2. Open the Menu, then Preferences, then AI Settings. Tip: type AI Settings in the Flexmls menu search box.
    3. Switch the MCP server toggle on.
    4. Read the terms and click I Agree.

    You should see: the MCP server toggle turned on.

    If you do not see AI Settings: realMLS already allows it, so update Flexmls or contact realMLS member support and ask them to enable the Flexmls MCP server for your login.

  3. Add RealMLS to your Claude account
    1. Open Claude connectors. Or in Claude click your name or initials at the bottom left, then Customize (older versions say Settings), then Connectors.
    2. Click the + button, then Add custom connector.
    3. Name: RealMLS Type it exactly like this. The studio looks for this name.
    4. Remote MCP server URL: https://mcp.fbsdata.com/mcp
    5. Leave Advanced settings alone and click Add.

    You should see: RealMLS in your connectors list with a Connect button.

    If there is no Add custom connector button: on a Team or Enterprise plan your Claude admin must allow custom connectors or add RealMLS for everyone. On the Free plan you can have only one custom connector, so remove any other custom connector first. If you already added it under a different name, remove it and add it again as RealMLS.

  4. Connect it with your Flexmls login
    1. Still in Claude connectors, click Connect next to RealMLS.
    2. A Flexmls sign in window opens. Sign in with your own Flexmls username and password. Claude never sees them.
    3. Click Allow if Flexmls asks.

    You should see: RealMLS marked as connected.

    If no window opens: allow pop ups for claude.ai in your browser and click Connect again. If Flexmls refuses: finish step 2 first. If it later says Sign in again: click RealMLS, then Reconnect.

  5. Let the studio use it
    1. Come back to the studio and reload the page so it sees your new connector.
    2. Open the CMA tab and click Set up everything.
    3. Claude asks if this page may use RealMLS. Click Allow. Choose Always allow if Claude offers it so you are not asked again.

    You should see: the checklist turn green and say All set.

    If you clicked Don't allow by mistake: reload the page and click Set up everything again. If nothing happens when you click: reload the page; the permission box only appears once per visit.

  6. Run the live test
    1. Click Test now in the checklist.

    You should see: Live test with your MLS login: Done.

    If it fails: the checklist names the reason. Flexmls refused: redo step 2. Sign in again: redo step 4. MLS busy: wait a minute and test again.

  7. Optional: turn on the online lookup
    1. In Claude connectors click Browse connectors.
    2. Search Parallel Search and click Connect. It is free and needs no login.
    3. Reload the studio and click Allow when asked.

    You should see: Online lookup: Done. Reports now fill gaps from public records, realtor.com, Redfin, Zillow, Homes.com, Trulia and PropertyReports.us.

    If it says the free search limit was reached: wait a few minutes. Reports still work with realMLS only.

                Make a 3D tour from a phone video
                
        
                3D scan apps turn a slow, steady phone video into a model of the home. From the model you can export a perfectly smooth fly through video, then bring it back here for iMath branding.
                
        
                AppCostGood for
                
          
                Luma 3D CaptureFreeWhole rooms; exports an MP4 fly through. Test it first, Luma now focuses on AI video.
                
          
                ScaniverseFree (iPhone)Single rooms and exteriors.
                
          
                PolycamFree plan with watermark, no splats. Basic about $150 a yearUnlimited 3D captures and clean video exports on Basic.
                
          
                MatterportOne tour free, then from about $14 a monthClassic dollhouse tours and floor plans.

                How to capture it
                
        
                1. Turn on every light, open blinds, close toilet lids and closets.
                2. Record in the scan app, wide lens, walking slowly at chest height. Overlap each area; circle big rooms once.
                3. Move through doorways slowly and straight. Keep people and pets out of the shot.
                4. Let the app process (often about as long as the video). Then export a fly through video (MP4).
                5. Upload the fly through below. The studio adds your intro, room labels, end card and the 3D disclosure.

                Photos for a listing tour
                • Shoot wide and level. Hold the phone straight up and down for vertical lines, at chest height.
                • Horizontal photos work for every format. The vertical video pans across them.
                • Order matters: front of home, entry, living, kitchen, dining, primary suite, other bedrooms, baths, outdoor spaces.
                • 15 to 25 photos make a 45 to 75 second tour. Two or three per big room is plenty.
                • Lights on, blinds open, counters cleared, cars out of the driveway.
                • No people, family photos, or personal items in the shot.
                • Virtually staged photos keep their "Virtually staged" tag on screen.
                
      
                Phone video walkthrough
                • Use a gimbal (about $100 to $150) or iPhone Action Mode. It is the biggest quality jump.
                • Hold the phone horizontal for YouTube and the MLS; vertical for Reels and TikTok. Pick one per video.
                • Walk slowly, about half your normal pace. Pause 2 seconds as you enter each room.
                • Glide, do not swing. Turn your body, not your wrist.
                • Start outside at the curb, end in the best spot: the backyard, the view or the kitchen.
                • Keep it under 3 minutes. The studio trims and speeds up hallways for you.
                • Mute the room: no talking. Add music or a voiceover here, or in your social app.
                
      
                Rules we follow
                • Motion only. No tool here adds, removes or changes anything in the home.
                • AI disclosure: photo tours carry "Animated with AI from listing photos." 3D fly throughs carry their own label. Follow Northeast Florida MLS rules for altered media; confirm current rules with the board before using a video in the MLS.
                • MLS version: virtual tour links in the MLS must usually be unbranded. Use the MLS version switch.
                • Other brokers' listings: only with permission. The end card names the listing office.
                • Fair Housing: describe the home, never the buyer. The studio checks your words before export.

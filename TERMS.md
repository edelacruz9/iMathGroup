# Terms of use

The Studio is a set of tools. You bring your own accounts, and you are responsible for them.

- **Your AI account.** You use your own Anthropic API key and pay Anthropic directly for what you use. Follow Anthropic's usage policies.
- **Your MLS account.** You connect your own MLS access through your own proxy. Follow your MLS's rules and your data license: what you may display, to whom, and with what attribution.
- **Photos and listings.** Only market listings and photos you have the right to use: your own listings, or ones where the listing broker and photographer gave permission.
- **Fair housing and disclosures.** Review everything the Studio generates before you publish it. Include your brokerage name, license number, and any required disclosures.
- **No data collection.** iMath runs no server for the Studio and does not receive your keys, your MLS data, or what you make. Your settings live only in your browser.

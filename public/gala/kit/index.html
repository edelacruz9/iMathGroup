Rules we follow

The coins

One coin per post, its own category only, the FCHCC gold coin on general posts. Never rotated, stretched, recolored, outlined or shadowed, and never with text on it. The light moves around the coin, not on it.

The words

No dashes or emojis. Spanish is written in Spanish with usted, accents included. Names on one line, spelled the way the person spells them.

Credit

The gala is the chamber's event; our posts support it and send people to its forms. Ticket and event detail posts carry Presented by VyStar Credit Union in small type. No VyStar logo, no VyStar tag.

Disclosure

Every end card carries the iMath Group logo with Powered by Cornerstone First Mortgage and NMLS 173855. Don't remove it or crop it out.

People

Post a nominee only after the chamber announces them or they say yes. Winner cards wait for the announcement in the room.

Dates and prices

Checked on the chamber's live site on Nov 7: nominations close Dec 2, gala Dec 17 from 6:00 to 9:00 PM at WJCT Studios, members $125, nonmembers $150, self nominations accepted.
